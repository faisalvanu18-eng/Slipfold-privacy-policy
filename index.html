Slipfold Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Slipfold is a single-player offline puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, hint and undo balances, and settings are saved locally on your device using Android app storage. We never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage, and may be included in your own Android device backup.

No account, no personal data collected by us

Slipfold has no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or files, and we do not run our own servers or analytics.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

After you clear level 10, the free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language), and ad interaction data. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. See Google's partner policy.

Your consent and choices

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, we use Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent, or let you opt out, before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

In-app purchase

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We receive only a purchase confirmation and never see your payment details.

Children

Slipfold is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower.

Security and retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies and is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update it in the app and at this address. Questions: hello@slipfold.app
